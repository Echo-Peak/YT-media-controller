# Privacy Policy for YTMediaControllerBrowser

**Effective date:** September 30, 2025

YTMediaControllerBrowser is a Chrome extension that opens a **local (localhost)
WebSocket** and listens to a companion app on your device for the sole purpose
of **playing videos**. We take privacy seriously and designed the extension to
operate **entirely on your device**.

## What data we collect

- **We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data.**
- We do not collect browsing history, page content, IP addresses, device
  identifiers, analytics, or telemetry.
- We do not use cookies or similar tracking technologies.

## How the extension works

- The extension opens a **localhost-only WebSocket** to receive commands or
  metadata from your locally running app.
- Communication remains on your device. **No data is sent to our servers or
  third parties.**

## Permissions

- The extension requests only the minimum Chrome permissions necessary to:
  - Open and maintain a **local WebSocket connection**.
  - Control video playback in tabs (when you explicitly use the extension).
- These permissions are used **exclusively** for core functionality; not for
  tracking.

## Third parties

- We do **not** share data with third parties.
- The Chrome Web Store or the Chrome browser may collect their own diagnostic or
  usage information under their respective policies; this is **outside our
  control**.

## Children’s privacy

- We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under
  13 (or applicable age of consent in your region).

## Security

- Because the extension does not transmit or store data off-device, risk is
  minimized.
- The localhost WebSocket is accessible only from your machine. We recommend
  keeping your system and browser up to date.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect product or legal changes. Updates will be
posted here with a new “Effective date.”
