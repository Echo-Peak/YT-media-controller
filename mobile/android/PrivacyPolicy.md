# Privacy Policy — YT Media Controller

**Effective date:** September 29, 2025  
**App package:** `com.ytmediacontroller.app`

## Summary

YT Media Controller uses the device **camera** only to scan QR codes for app
setup. We do **not** collect, store, or share personal data.

## Permissions and Purpose

- **Camera (`android.permission.CAMERA`)** — used exclusively to scan QR codes
  you present to the app.
- **Network access** — used only to communicate with endpoints you configure
  (e.g., your local or cloud backend).

## Data Collection and Sharing

- **Collection:** None by default.
- **Storage:** No photos or videos are saved or uploaded by the app.
- **Sharing/Sale:** None.

## Third-Party Libraries

Open-source libraries (e.g., QR scanning, networking) may be included. These
libraries run locally and do not transmit data off-device except as required for
the feature you initiate.

## Security

Connections to your configured endpoints use the protocols you provide (e.g.,
HTTPS/TLS). You are responsible for securing those endpoints.

## Children’s Privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
personal information from children.

## Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by updating the
**Effective date** above.
